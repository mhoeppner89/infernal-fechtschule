Forward and back follow your facing at the Guard tap. Complete a guard command within 450 ms. A fresh follow-up waits up to 260 ms; confirmed light hits link sooner. Holding Cut never repeats. A simultaneous Cut + Finish chord chooses Cut. These route names describe the longsword; the dussack uses shorter one-handed variants.

Routes: direction + STEP dodges · neutral STEP → CUT / FINISH attacks low; directional STEP → CUT makes a passing cut · GUARD + direction → CUT · PARRY → CUT / FINISH.

When an item is marked on the road, SWAP changes to TAKE. Release the guide to return to the fight.
